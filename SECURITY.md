# Security policy

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to **security@rumormill.ai**. Include the affected
URL or component, reproduction steps, impact, and any supporting evidence. Do not include secrets or
unnecessary personal data.

We will acknowledge reports within three business days, provide a triage update within seven
business days, and coordinate remediation and disclosure. Please do not open a public issue before
we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and fix the problem.

The currently deployed MVP is the supported version. Security fixes are applied to that deployment;
older commits and local development installations are not separately supported.
