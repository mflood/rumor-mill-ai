A visitor’s guide to Greyhaven

How to play The Lighthouse

The Lighthouse is a living mystery made for reading, exploring, and private conversation. You do not need an account, and this guide works even when Greyhaven is between seasons.

What should I do next?

Follow the story loop.

  1. Read Today. Start with the latest published story update visible to everyone.
  2. Explore the Town map. Choose a place and see what is publicly happening there.
  3. Contact a resident privately. Open People, check the resident’s private-contact status, and send a message when contact is available.
  4. Return later. The town clock may move and Greyhaven may change while you are away.

What can change while I am away?

Greyhaven keeps its own town clock.

The clock in the page header shows the story’s current day and time. As it moves, residents may change public whereabouts, events may unfold, private replies may arrive, and a new public story update may be published. If the season or clock is paused, the page says so and its displayed state remains the last confirmed one.

What is public versus private?

Published story, public whereabouts, private words.

Public story updates
A dispatch is a published story update visible to everyone. Today shows the latest update; Archive keeps updates that have already been published.
Public whereabouts
The Town map shows only where a resident is publicly known to be. Public absence never means that private contact is unavailable.
Private conversations
Only you and the resident can read your exchange. Conversation text never appears in Town, Today, or Archive.

When and how can I contact a resident?

Check People before you write.

A resident’s profile gives the current private-contact mode. A conversation may be live, asynchronous, delayed, or unavailable. This is separate from public whereabouts: someone away from the Town map may still accept a private message. When contact is unavailable, the profile tells you to return after a later town update.

What does a resident remember about me?

Your visit notes belong to this visit.

Residents can remember useful details from the private conversations they shared with you. People shows your relationship summary and recent remembered exchanges. A resident does not receive another visitor’s conversation, and your private conversation is not added to the public story.

What is in Archive?

Published public story updates, in season order.

Each Archive episode is one published story update. It may contain several story dispatches that present parts of that update. Archive does not contain unfinished material, hidden character knowledge, or private conversations.

What if something is unavailable?

Use the explanation where you stopped.

  • No season: no story is progressing. Core story routes appear when Greyhaven is available; previous published seasons may remain in Archive.
  • Quiet location: follow that page’s suggested public activity, resident, or honest wait action.
  • Resident unavailable: private contact cannot start now; return after the next town update.
  • Story update unavailable: Today or Archive explains whether publication is still being prepared, can be retried safely, or has not happened yet.

The message on the affected page is the current source of truth. This guide does not replace its state-specific next step.
